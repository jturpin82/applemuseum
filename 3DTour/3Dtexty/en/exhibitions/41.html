3 MacBook "Unibody"

4 MacBook Air 13

The new version of the MacBook Air created not only a surprise in the form of a smaller 11" version, but also an innovative 13.3" variant. Because these MacBook Airs don’t use flash drives, they can be narrower and have bigger battery compartments.

5 MacBook Pro Retina

Apple’s marketing department wanted to show that they were completely migrating to Intel platforms, so they decided to bid farewell to the well-known and respected brand PowerBook. Thus the MacBook Pro was created. It is visually different in two ways. The webcam looks like a black rectangle over the screen; the front houses a round receiver for the remote control. The MacBook Pro Retina was the first with a so-called retina display, i.e. a screen with excellent resolution.
